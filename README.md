## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** as part of the Decode Labs Data Analytics Industrial Training Program (Project 4).

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through visual storytelling, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance, identify trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze yearly sales performance.
- Track revenue and order trends.
- Monitor order statuses.
- Compare product performance.
- Understand customer referral effectiveness.
- Evaluate payment method preferences.
- Detect revenue outliers.

---

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

The dashboard displays key performance indicators:

- Total Orders
- Average Order Value
- Total Revenue
- Outlier Count

---

### 📅 Year Slicer

Interactive filtering for:

- 2023
- 2024
- 2025

Users can instantly switch between years to analyze changing business trends.

---

### 📊 Monthly Sales Trend

Visualizes monthly performance to identify:

- Peak sales periods
- Low-performing months
- Revenue fluctuations

Insights:
- 2023 showed strong performance in May.
- 2024 peaked during June.
- 2025 recorded lower sales volume due to partial-year data.

---

### 🔄 Order Status Analysis

Tracks order distribution across:

- Delivered
- Shipped
- Pending
- Returned
- Cancelled

This helps monitor operational efficiency and customer fulfillment.

---

### 🛒 Product Revenue Analysis

Compares revenue generated by products including:

- Chair
- Desk
- Laptop
- Monitor
- Printer
- Tablet
- Phone

Key Findings:

#### 2023
- Printer generated the highest revenue.

#### 2024
- Chair emerged as the top-selling product.

#### 2025
- Desk became the leading revenue contributor.

---

### 📢 Referral Source Analysis

Measures Average Order Value by acquisition channel:

- Facebook
- Instagram
- Google
- Email
- Referral

Insights:
- Facebook consistently delivered higher-value customers.
- Referral channels remained effective across years.

---

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

Revenue contribution by payment methods:

- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- Cash
- Gift Card
- Online Payments

This helps businesses optimize payment experiences.
